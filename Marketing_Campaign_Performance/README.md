# 📢 ShopSphere — Marketing & Campaign Performance Dashboard

> **Measuring marketing investment, customer acquisition, conversions, and channel performance through interactive Power BI analytics.**

---

## 📌 Overview

The **Marketing & Campaign Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed as part of the ShopSphere E-Commerce Analytics project.

The dashboard evaluates the effectiveness of ShopSphere's marketing activities by analyzing **campaign costs, customer acquisition, conversions, conversion rates, and revenue generated through different acquisition channels**.

It provides a consolidated view of marketing performance and helps identify which acquisition channels are contributing most effectively to the business.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to understand the relationship between **marketing investment and customer acquisition performance**.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How much is ShopSphere spending on marketing campaigns?
- How many customers have been acquired?
- How many conversions have been generated?
- What is the overall conversion rate?
- Which acquisition channels generate the most conversions?
- Which channels generate the highest revenue?
- How does campaign cost vary across acquisition channels?
- Which marketing channels should receive greater attention?

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

A line chart compares campaign spending across different acquisition channels.

The channels analyzed include:

- Referral
- Email
- Instagram
- Organic Search

This visualization helps identify channels associated with higher or lower marketing expenditure.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides filters for:

- **Campaign Date**
- **Marketing Campaign**
- **Acquisition Channel**

These filters allow users to analyze marketing performance for specific campaign periods, campaigns, or acquisition channels.

For example, management can select a specific campaign and evaluate its associated cost, conversions, customers acquired, and revenue contribution.
